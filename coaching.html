How it works

What working together looks like

Coaching with me is a conversation, not a curriculum. We start with what's actually going on — the thing you'd say out loud if nobody were keeping score — and work from there.

Sessions are unhurried. I'll ask questions rather than hand you answers, because the goal isn't for you to leave dependent on my judgement. It's for you to leave trusting your own.

Between sessions you'll have practices to sit with and a line open to me. Progress usually looks less like a breakthrough and more like a series of small, honest decisions that start pointing the same direction.

Who it's for

  • You're doing well on paper and it isn't landing
  • You're facing a decision you keep circling
  • You've outgrown a version of yourself but haven't replaced it
  • You want a thinking partner, not a cheerleader
  • You're willing to be honest — with me and with yourself
