My story

The long way round

I didn't set out to become a coach. I set out to answer a question I couldn't shake: why does life feel so heavy when, on paper, everything is fine?

For years I chased what I was supposed to want — the role, the milestones, the version of success that made sense to everyone else. From the outside it looked like it was working. Inside, I felt further from myself than I'd ever been.

What changed wasn't a plan. It was learning to listen — slowing down enough to hear what I actually wanted, and trusting that voice even when it didn't make sense yet. That work is still ongoing. It isn't a place you arrive at.

Now I sit with people standing at the same crossroads. Not to hand them a map, but to help them find the one they've been carrying all along.
