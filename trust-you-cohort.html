Trust You

Starts 5 October 2026 · 8-week live online cohort

You can be surrounded by people who love you and still feel like nobody really knows you.

Trust You is an 8-week live cohort for people who are tired of performing in their closest relationships. By the end of the cohort you will stop self-abandoning and trust yourself to be fully seen as who you really are.

Stop earning your place in your relationships. Start trusting you already have one.

Does this sound familiar?

Most of this never gets said out loud.

  • You shrink in rooms and hold back what you actually think.
  • You say yes when you mean no — and when you can't say no, you avoid the person instead.
  • You rehearse the conversation for three days and never have it.
  • You need to know how it'll go before you walk in.
  • If someone asked what you actually want, you'd struggle to answer.
  • You're the reliable one. The easy one. The one who's always fine.

You're loved. You're just not known.

Why I built this

I loved people. I just never felt safe being myself around them.

So I shrank in rooms. I hid what I thought. I couldn't say no, so I'd avoid people rather than disappoint them — the thought of letting someone down was unbearable.

I was certain that if I told the truth about who I actually was, nobody would understand, and they'd leave.

I never considered what I wanted. Not "I ignored my wants" — I genuinely didn't know what they were. And the caring, sensitive part of me, the best part, I treated as a weakness to hide.

I was surrounded by people who loved me and I have never felt so alone.

This cohort is the way out that I had to build for myself.

Who it's for

This is for you if

  • Your life looks fine from the outside and something in your closest relationships feels hollow.
  • You avoid conflict and call it being easygoing.
  • You over-give until you're empty, then feel guilty for being tired.
  • You're willing to be seen while you're still working it out.

This isn't for you if

  • You're in crisis or an unsafe relationship — that needs proper support, not a group cohort.
  • You're here to change someone else. This only works on your side.
  • You want to watch it alone with your camera off.

The format

8 weeks. Live. Online. A small group.

  • 7 live group workshops and 7 live group connection sessions — days and times advised closer to the start date
  • Weekly experiments and accountability between sessions
  • A community page with the materials, recordings, and a place to connect
  1. Week 1 1:1 call with me
  2. Week 2 The Mask of Performance — the internal programming running your life
  3. Week 3 The Experimental Mindset — how to build self-trust
  4. Week 4 The Daily Practice — managing anxiety and creating internal safety
  5. Week 5 The Kingdom — reparenting your parts and the golden algorithm
  6. Week 6 Projections — changing the way you show up in your relationships
  7. Week 7 The Dreamer — connect to your inner guide
  8. Week 8 Taking Off The Mask — closing call

The group is the point, not the packaging. You can't learn to be seen on your own. Eight weeks of saying true things in front of people who started as strangers is the practice — for every relationship in your life.
