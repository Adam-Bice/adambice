Trust You Cohort

A small group container for people who are done outsourcing their answers — and ready to learn what their own judgement sounds like.

What it is

Learning to trust yourself, together

Most of us learned early to look outward for direction — to the people who seemed sure, the advice that sounded sensible, the path that made everyone else comfortable. It works, until it doesn't.

Trust You is a season-long cohort for unlearning that. We meet regularly as a small group, work through the places where you've handed your authority away, and practise something quieter and more durable: listening to yourself and acting on what you hear.

It's deliberately small. The point isn't a curriculum you consume — it's a group of people who know your story well enough to notice when you're drifting from it.

What's included

  • Regular live group sessions over the length of the cohort
  • A small, committed group — capped intentionally low
  • Practices and prompts to carry into the weeks between
  • An ongoing space to check in between sessions
  • Direct access to me throughout
